## E-commerce Revolution — Aurora Skincare
**Website Development · 4 months**

### The Challenge
An ageing e-commerce platform with slow load times and a confusing checkout.
Traffic was growing but sales were falling, and cart abandonment sat at 78%.

### Our Solution
A full rebuild centred on user experience, performance, and conversion:
mobile-first design, a streamlined checkout, better product discovery, and
proper analytics instrumentation from day one.

### What We Shipped
- Mobile-optimised responsive design
- Advanced product filtering and search
- One-click checkout process
- Integrated inventory management
- Customer review system
- Email marketing automation

### Results
| Metric | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion Rate | +340% | From 1.2% to 5.3% |
| Page Speed | +85% | Faster load times |
| Cart Abandonment | −45% | From 78% to 43% |
| Revenue | 2.1x | Year-over-year growth |

### Client Quote
> "The new site exceeded what we asked for. It looks the part, and the numbers
> moved within the first month."
> — Founder, Aurora Skincare

**Stack & Channels:** Shopify, Custom Design, Performance, UX/UI

## PPC Performance Boost — Meridian Legal
**Performance Marketing · 3 months**

### The Challenge
A significant monthly Google Ads budget producing mostly unqualified leads,
with cost-per-acquisition far above what the practice could sustain.

### Our Solution
A full campaign restructure — keyword research, aggressive negative keyword
work, audience segmentation, rewritten ad copy, landing page testing, and
disciplined bid management.

### What We Shipped
- Advanced keyword and intent targeting
- Location-based campaign optimisation
- Landing page A/B testing
- Conversion tracking setup
- Audience remarketing campaigns
- Competitor analysis and positioning

### Results
| Metric | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Cost Per Acquisition | −65% | Against the prior baseline |
| Qualified Leads | +300% | Monthly, post-restructure |
| ROAS | 8.2x | Return on ad spend |
| Click-Through Rate | +180% | Improved ad relevance |

### Client Quote
> "Our costs dropped while the quality of enquiries went up. That combination
> is what we had been told was impossible."
> — Managing Partner, Meridian Legal

**Stack & Channels:** Google Ads, Meta Ads, Analytics, CRO
